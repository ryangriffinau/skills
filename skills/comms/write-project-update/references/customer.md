# Customer mode

Use for a named customer, client or external sponsor. The reader knows their
operation, not the repository.

## Keep

- Address a named person when the channel and context support it.
- Lead with the shared asks block whenever action is pending.
- Order updates by what the reader sees in their work.
- Translate implementation into what they stop doing by hand, stop missing or
  can now trust.
- Keep rollout ownership clear: state readiness, and leave go-live or staff
  exposure decisions with the customer when those decisions are theirs.
- Use at most one plumbing sentence in the main update. Put valuable technical
  depth in the optional appendix.

## Cut

Delete internal churn: refactors, item totals, ceremonies, backlog clearing,
deployment plumbing and any sentence whose subject is the delivery team's
effort. Keep operational caveats that affect the customer.

## Shape

1. Direct opener, when useful
2. Actions upfront, when any ask exists
3. Updates, ordered by customer visibility
4. Coming next, limited to relevant committed or in-flight work

_Mode check:_ every main-section item changes what this reader knows, trusts,
does or decides.
