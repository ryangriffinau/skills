# Internal mode

Use for a delivery team, leadership group or project owner who can act on status,
risk and trade-offs.

## Keep

- Lead with decisions, approvals, access or owner actions that unblock work.
- Report outcome, status, risk and next step compactly.
- Name owners and dates when the source supports them.
- Cite work-item IDs and metrics in the copy when they help the audience act.
- Separate shipped, in progress, blocked and next work.
- State uncertainty and temporary degradation directly.

## Cut

Remove narrative chronology, ceremonial progress, duplicated tracker detail and
technical context that does not affect a decision, risk or dependency.

## Shape

1. Decisions and actions needed
2. Outcome summary
3. Shipped / in progress / blocked
4. Risks and mitigations
5. Next milestones

_Mode check:_ every section changes a decision, owner, priority, risk assessment
or shared understanding of delivery state.
